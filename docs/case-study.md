# Sample case study

## Decision question

Which operational ideas merit a structured discovery conversation before a team commits to a prototype?

## Approach

The sample register compares four fictional ideas using the same transparent inputs. The tool validates the register, calculates a weighted screening score, makes the assumed annual time figure visible, and surfaces sensitivity or risk ratings for discussion.

## What this example demonstrates

- A consistent way to compare unlike ideas without hiding subjective assumptions.
- Clear separation between estimated operational benefit, readiness, feasibility, and risk.
- A short output that can be reviewed by technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- A practical handoff from an initial concept to a discovery checklist.

## Limits

The sample has no measured baseline, production data, user research, cost model, or legal review. No conclusion about real-world benefit or compliance should be drawn from the synthetic rows.
